01 / Architecture

Research is separate from production ranking.

The lab tests factor definitions and applies promotion or rejection rules. The ranking engine uses only the factors and weights already approved.

01 / TEST

Research lab

Measures factor efficacy, checks robustness and artifacts, compares universes, and applies promotion or rejection rules.

→
02 / APPLY

Ranking engine

Combines approved signals at predefined weights into a monthly sector-neutral ranking of the investable universe.

→
03 / INVESTIGATE

Research workflow

Uses the ranking to decide which companies to research first. It does not produce buy instructions.

03 / Decision register

Five changes were fixed before the final run.

The decisions were set on 30 September 2026 and were not changed after the final verdict run began.

Quality → zero

The broad result did not transfer to large caps. The exact eight-metric sleeve produced 21-day IC +0.039 (t 5.17) across the broad panel, but large-cap IC was approximately zero. It remains scored. Reinstatement requires positive trailing-12-month large-cap IC with t > 2, reviewed at least annually—not a single favorable window.

Low-vol → 126d realized vol

The beta definition produced negative returns. The 252-day beta-versus-SPY definition returned −9.4%/yr on the broad panel and −7.8%/yr on large caps. The earlier 126-day realized-volatility definition returned as the 0.10 sleeve; beta stays diagnostic.

Value → earnings yield

Earnings yield was more stable and tested better. Earnings yield (1/P/E) replaces P/E at 0.20 weight. Standalone testing favored earnings yield: IC +0.037 (t 3.93) versus +0.007 (t 0.69) for P/E, while avoiding P/E’s instability near zero earnings.

Size → zero

The broad result did not hold under native large-cap scoring. A +11.2%/yr diagnostic spread became −1.4%/yr when size was scored natively among large caps. The sleeve remains visible with a documented flip-back rule; it no longer affects production rank.

Top quintile → cap-weighted, long-only

The short book was not investable under the stated assumptions. It was concentrated in low-priced small-cap names, including roughly 30% sub-$5 names during the failure window. A flat 10 bps model could not represent borrow. Shorting was cut; Q5 is capitalization-weighted and assessed against SPY with 10 bps one-way transaction costs.

05 / Data & method

Point-in-time data, fixed rules, and explicit tests.

The pipeline checks for look-ahead leakage, missing data, and invalid values before factors are evaluated.

Data discipline

  • LICENSED INPUTSIntrinio bulk prices, fundamentals and security metadata. Raw licensed files are not distributed.
  • ~17M PRICE ROWSHistorical coverage spans roughly 22,569 tickers; the research panel used 80 monthly trade dates from February 2020 through September 2026.
  • PUBLICATION-TIME JOINFundamentals enter only once publicly available, using merge_asof on filing or calculable date—not fiscal period end.
  • NO SURVIVORSHIP FILTERThe broad panel is built from historical vendor records, not today’s index constituents.

Scoring discipline

  • WINSORIZE ±3σCross-sectional raw metrics are clipped before standardization to reduce outlier leverage.
  • SECTOR-NEUTRAL Z-SCORESSignals are standardized within sector so industry structure does not dominate rank.
  • QUARANTINE INVALID MULTIPLESZero or negative P/E, P/S, P/B and EV/EBITDA are never ranked as “cheapest.”
  • RENORMALIZE PER TICKERAvailable sleeve weights are rescaled so missing data does not mechanically depress a company’s composite.
compositeᵢ = Σ(wₛ × zᵢ,ₛ) / Σ(wₛ available for ticker i)

Current configured weights: value 0.20 · momentum 0.20 · low-volatility (126d) 0.10. Effective fully-observed mix: 0.40 / 0.40 / 0.20.

07 / Limitations

Limits of the result.

The negative verdict is specific to the tested data, universe, horizon and implementation. It is not proof that factor investing cannot work.

In-sample decisions

Sleeve weights and definitions were chosen after seeing research from the same 2020–2026 window. Promotion requires a genuinely forward test, not another re-cut of this sample.

Restated fundamentals

Filing dates control availability, but vendor fundamentals may reflect later restatements. That can leave residual look-ahead contamination in historical values.

One regime window

February 2020 through September 2026 contains a pandemic crash, speculative rebound and mega-cap-led market. It is not a full market history.

Licensed data excluded

Raw Intrinio bulk files are not included in the repository. Code, configuration and compact result summaries can be reviewed; full reproduction requires licensed inputs.

The ranking is an attention filter, not a buy list.
